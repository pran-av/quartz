a [[Mineral]]. One of the most common minerals on Earth.

![[QuartzStructure.png| 450]]
#### Composition
- Silicon Dioxide SiO2
- Trace elements of Al, Li, Ge

#### Physical Properties
1. Chemically stable
2. Resistant to weathering
3. Inert - does not easily react with other substances

#### Chemical Properties
1. Hard - ranks 7 in Mohs hardness test for minerals
2. Some varieties are transparent or coloured based on trace element composition
3. [[Piezoelectricity]] - generates electric charge when subjected to mechanical stress
4. High melting point of about 1600 degree Celsius

#### Applications
1. Production of oscillators and frequency control devices due to piezoelectricity
2. Aggregate in concrete, production of glass and ceramics
3. Jewelry
4. Optics - used in lenses and prisms
