the company I want to build will have following goals,

1. to bring efficiency in delivery of day to day social needs of citizens, like food, water, housing, healthcare, travel, justice, safety, housing services, and community services. The goal may not include direct engagement in delivery of the above products or services, but to facilitate the scale or adoption of these products/services through technology solutions. The delivering agents might include government agencies or public, private entities, to whom this company aims to serve as an enabler. The technology solutions may include consumer or internal software tools or hardware products.
2. to rejuvenate the cultural values that lead to artistic breakthroughs. The art may be in the science, architecture, sports, storytelling, management or in philosophy (way of thought). The longterm technology solutions in this field can revolve around way of education, access to resources, ability to distribute, or infrastructure.
3. to create wealth and purpose within the society, the technology solutions here will be around exploring new industries like space, artificial intelligence, manufacturing that can later become big drivers for the economy employing large number of citizens.

##### **A. Main Objects to be Pursued by the Company**

1. **To facilitate efficiency in the delivery of essential social services** such as food, water, housing, healthcare, travel, justice, safety, and community services, by providing enabling technology solutions. The Company shall focus on developing, implementing, and scaling innovative consumer-facing software tools, internal management systems, or hardware products for governmental, public, or private entities engaged in the delivery of these services.

2. **To rejuvenate cultural values and drive artistic breakthroughs** by creating and promoting technology-enabled solutions that empower education, access to resources, and infrastructure development in fields such as science, architecture, sports, storytelling, management, and philosophy. The Company shall work on long-term technology solutions that transform education, resource sharing, and distribution channels, thus fostering a culture of innovation and creativity.

3. **To drive societal wealth creation and economic purpose** by identifying and developing technology solutions in emerging sectors such as space exploration, artificial intelligence, advanced manufacturing, and other future industries. The Company will focus on building enabling platforms, tools, and infrastructure that accelerate the growth of these industries and contribute to large-scale employment opportunities.
