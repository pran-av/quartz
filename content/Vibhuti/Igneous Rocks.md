Igneous rocks are formed through crystallisation process while cooling of magma
### Types based on formation

#### Plutonic Rocks (Intrusive)
cooled and solidify below the crust
- [[Granite]]
- Diorite
- Gabbro
- Peridotite

#### Volcanic Rocks (Extrusive)
cooled and solidify on the surface
- Rhyolite
- Andesite
- Bassalt
- Komatite

### Types based on Silica content

#### Felsic Rocks
High Silica content over 65%
- Granite
- Rhyolite
#### Intermediate Rocks
55 to 65% Silica content
- Diorite
- Andesite
#### Mafic Rocks
45 to 55% Silica content
- Gabbro
- Basalt
#### Ultramafic Rocks
less than 45% Silica content
- Peridotite
- Komatite

### Types based on Mineral content
vary based on feldspar, quartz, olivine, pyroxene content -- composition affects colour and density

### Types based on Elemental content
vary based on iron, magnesium, calcium and potassium content
