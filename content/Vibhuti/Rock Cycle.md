the elements of a rock cycle include,
1. [[Igneous Rocks]]: Formed by cooled and crystallised magma
2. Sedimentary Rocks: Formed by compaction of sediments
3. Metamorphic Rocks: Formed by application of heat and pressure on sedimentary rocks
4. Sediments: All rocks turn to sediments due to weathering

### Lithification
compaction of sediments leading to sedimentary rock formation

### Metamorphism
alterations within existing rocks under heat and pressure

### Weathering
erosion of rocks

### Melting
formation of magma
