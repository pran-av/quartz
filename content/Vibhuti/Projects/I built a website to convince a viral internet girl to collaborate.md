The chronology of events that took place from this idea hitting me to having its website deployed to production was pretty fast. If I would have spent at-least 5 minutes thinking deeper on this idea - I would have certainly called it silly and dumped it.

## Here's the context,

>[!info] **Chronology of Events**
>1. A girl posts a 3 second video on X, which goes super viral
>2. There are news articles calling her Bandana Girl
>3. It reaches 25 million plus impressions on X
>4. I get a random thought if bandanagirl.com has been purchased
>5. I quickly check - yes it was purchased. Website suggests me bandanagirl.in is available only for ~500 INR
>6. I purchase it, while buying I think what to do with it. Shall I just keep it or build something on it
>7. Then I browse X and see its going super viral hitting close to 30 million impressions now
>8. I think this girl ought to end up being a big personal brand with that virality
>9. What if I just pitch her to collaborate and build something together. I got a perfect brand, if I convince her to join we might be able to sell almost anything
>10. I think the goal is to be rich - this ain't a startup, this is a hack. Learning the behaviour around us and hacking reality to earn something out of it.
>11. I open cursor write a quick PRD - and within half an hour of back and forth -- The design, the pitch, the call to action all figured out
>12. Deployed the website through Netlify and configured DNS onto Cloudflare. Pointed CNAME back to Cloudflare and now the website was running on **[bandanagirl.in](https://www.bandanagirl.in)**

## The website

I think I got a pretty decent one made quick,

2. The body of messaging is not as good maybe because we do not know her viewpoint -- 'earning revenues/being rich' -> maybe she does not care, or else she could have monetised her X. 'building something together' -> maybe she is already building something with another partner
3. Putting some ideas as cards was a good touch to help communicate what I meant
4. And the CTA was my X profile - where one can check for authenticity and DM
5. I did not use any framework for the website - simple HTML, CSS and a Server.JS file
