# Roadmap for Piqsi

## Phase One

### Onboarding
1. **Login or Signup** with Phone Number
2. **Student Profile Creation** - based on track
3. Welcome with **Enrolment Summary**
4. Brief **Walkthrough of Features**

### Ask Piqsi
1. **Ask Questions and get Answers** - academics or general knowledge
2. **Frequent Actions** - simplifying academic operations for students by providing them tools
    1. **Manage Tasks**: The tool helps the student add or remove tasks for any day. Summarise To-Dos everyday and mark tasks as completed or pending.
    2. **Manage Events**: The tool helps student to discover on campus events or off campus academic, sport, or cultural events.
    3. **Summarise Documents**: The tool allows student to attach pdf documents or photos, summarise them or convert into notes.
    4. **Fetch Job Opportunities**: The tool helps students analyse their resume for quality and share suggestion. It also provides job suggestions that suit their resumes.
    5. **Practice Interviews**: The tool helps user practice for interviews by providing them scenarios to discuss upon - guesstimate, strategy, or domain based questions.
3. **Bookmark** Answers to visit later. Share answers.

### Academic Dashboard
1. **Attendance and Assignment Submission Summary** - student can generate a study plan based on pending assignments
2. **Notice Board/Alerts** - notifications from faculty or institution, submission due alerts
3. **Time Table** - displays daily timetable and allows students to ask specific questions for future schedules
4. **Payment Summary** - displays pending and upcoming payments, student can view the payment structure
5. **Ask administration** - student can ask questions specific to adminstration like related to campus, hostel, fee structure, faculties, classrooms, time table etc

### Upskilling Dashboard
1. **Skillset Summary** - Includes skillsets acquired by students through courses, ranks skillsets with collegues
2. **Skill Gap Analysis** - Mentions missing skills necessary for the student's track
3. **Suggested Courses** - Suggestion for course curated by faculty to test skillsets or bridge the skill gaps
4. **Job Panel** - Mentions number of job applications, job suggestion based on match percentage from acquired skilsets through courses, resume analysis and suggestions

### Campus Connect
1. **Discover Clubs, Events and Workshops** - student can view details of all activities and their participants
2. **View Participant Profiles** - students can view basic details like Name, Track, and Institution Email ID from student profiles
3. **Create Activities** - a student can create activities and request authourization if in campus. Sport or other off campus activities with guardrails.
4. **Alumni Connect** - A student can request connection with an alumni by entering their requirement. Alumni profiles can be suggested for mock interviews, experience sharing, etc
