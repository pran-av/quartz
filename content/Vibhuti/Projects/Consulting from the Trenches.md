## Go To Market

### **Target Market**
1. Companies with Zero to One goals
2. Indie Hackers looking to outsource non core tasks

### **Need**
1. Optimise operational costs: Want to get work done at lesser costs than hiring
2. Improve internal productivity by letting non core tasks be handled outside the company
3. Improve success rate: Outsource experience from outside when lacking internally

### **Avoid**
1. Any big development work which will delay timelines and risk losing momentum
2. Building a team, instead can always take advise from them. If clients are specifically looking for team members with a fixed set of experience, can figure out then. Maybe I'll relay it to another consultant with sales cost.
3. Main goal is to build products of our own, so avoid services beyond cash flow creation mechanism
4. Do not build an agency, this is supposed to be a consultancy

### **USP**
1. Vision to be in the trenches with them
2. A mixed skillset of product, engineering, and sales that would matter for a zero to one company

### **What will they pay for?**
Companies who wish to hire consultants would look for experience, references and pricing.

#### **Experience**

#### **Pricing**

### **Content Strategy**

**Quantified frameworks** and **cases** in the form of **shorts** that are delivered with design that instills **confidence and trust**.

**Frameworks**
Having good first principles is my strong point - I should write about frameworks, how to tackle a problem, how to implement a solution, how to track and reiterate.

**Cases**
I already have some personal projects built - I need to break them down into multiple problem statements to define how we solved them.

**Shorts**
Readers have less time to read and lesser attention - best to have 3-4 paragraph blinks/shots instead of essays. Great if readers can swipe through them like inshorts.

**Quantity**
Quantification is the most important - its easy to grasp and conclude its value. Also it reduces the number of words.

**Trust and Confidence**
Intelligent design - necessary to generate trust, confidence, creativity in the reader
