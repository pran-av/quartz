## Factors Involved

To make the research more precise, we need to consider certain factors that impact the need for travel planning services and tools. These factors need further distribution with country, because as culture changes the variables for these factors change. The factors are,
1. Population of the country
2. Individuals who travel annually
3. Number of trips annually (can consider per individual, a family trip will be considered as 4 trips)
4. Average spends on planning per trip (changes further based on purpose of the trip)
5. Individual who prefer not planning
