As far as I remember, I have always been a mediocre, just good enough to keep going. I was okay being a mediocre kid, a mediocre teenager and I had no concept of mediocrity until some part of my adult life. I had no concept of greatness either. And then it just appeared one fine day - possibly by looking at people around me. Someone who is great at sports, somebody who is part of a premier institute, someone who's part of a well known brand.

Those brands are not just names - they are a symbol of grit, discipline, persistence and all those qualities that makes a Great society.

From that subconscious reflection, the concept of greatness was born in me. Being Great means, you are a propellant to the society. You contribute those qualities that keeps a society alive and running. People around you see great work and aspire to be part of that Greatness, they hope one day they will be known to be great at something as well, one thing leads to another and a new cycle of pursuit to Greatness begins. If there's enough Great work and Great people around us, our culture and society gets contagious enough that it reaches that excitation stage where the organisation finds its escape velocity into higher and higher orbits.

Achieving this greatness requires energy, energy requires fuel - the pursuit to greatness also requires some fuel. The fuel is sometimes a form of curiosity, sometimes its a subconscious want, sometimes its a logical necessity, or sometimes we just don't know what that fuel is however we can feel something driving us ahead.

At this point, I don't think its worth worrying about the fuel much, what if we exhaust the fuel? There's no fuel-gauge to keep a track of and not having one can add up to our anxiety or we become more calculative and go into an unnecessary preservation mode. When we finally understand what that fuel is, we can start worrying, but I just feel that its not something that can easily deplete. Like they say, we are born out of stardust, we were all part of a star once, so let's believe that speck of star in us will give us all energies we need to achieve our greatness.
