In 2024, India witnessed a total of approximately **2.61 crore units** (26.1 million) sold in the automobile market, marking a **9.1% increase** from the previous year. Here’s a detailed breakdown of automobile sales by type:

## Breakdown of Automobile Sales in India (2024)

| Type of Automobile               | Units Sold       | Year-over-Year Change |
|----------------------------------|------------------|-----------------------|
| **Two-Wheelers**                 | 1.63 crore (16.3 million) | +10.7%                |
| **Passenger Vehicles**           | 43 lakh (4.3 million)      | +5.1%                 |
| **Three-Wheelers**               | 57 lakh (5.7 million)      | +10.4%                |
| **Commercial Vehicles**          | 8.5 lakh (0.85 million)    | +0.07%                |
| **Tractors**                     | 7 lakh (0.7 million)       | +2.5%                 |
| **Total**                        | 2.61 crore (26.1 million)  | +9.1%                 |

### Market Dynamics
The growth in automobile sales was influenced by several factors, including:
- Increased rural consumption and favorable monsoon conditions. (Why is there a rural shift?)
- A strong shift towards SUVs in the passenger vehicle category. (Why is there an SUV shift?)
- Continued recovery from pandemic-related disruptions.

In 2024, the Indian passenger vehicle market achieved significant sales milestones, with a total of **43 lakh units** sold. Here’s the breakdown of passenger car sales by brand:

## Passenger Car Sales by Brand in India (2024)

| Brand                   | Units Sold | Year-over-Year Change |

| **Toyota Kirloskar**    | 3,26,329   | +40%                  |
| **Kia**                 | 2,55,038   | +6%                   |

### Key Insights
- **Maruti Suzuki**: Continued to dominate the market with nearly **18 lakh units sold**, maintaining its position as the largest car manufacturer in India.
- **Hyundai**: Achieved impressive growth with over **6 lakh units sold**, driven significantly by the popularity of its SUV models.
- **Tata Motors**: Recorded sales of approximately **5.65 lakh units**, benefiting from strong demand for its SUVs and electric vehicles.
- **Toyota Kirloskar**: Sold around **3.26 lakh units**, marking a notable increase in sales driven by the success of models like the Innova.
- **Kia**: Reached sales of about **2.55 lakh units**, reflecting steady growth in its offerings.

This data highlights the competitive landscape of the Indian passenger vehicle market in 2024, showcasing how brands are adapting to consumer preferences and market dynamics.

| **Total**                        | 15,954,053       | +2.1%                 |

- The U.S. automotive market has seen a shift towards light trucks over the past two decades, with these vehicles now making up a significant majority of total sales. (Why?)
