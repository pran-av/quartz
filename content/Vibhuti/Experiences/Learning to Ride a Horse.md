### Day 1

#### Walking Embrosia (the horse) in circles
1. **Hold:** Hold the horse by the Rein (ref image below). Hold at a natural rein position, don't pull the rein down or up.
2. **Standing Position while Walking**: Stand close and parallel to the horse's face -- allows leg room for horse to walk with you
3. **Walking the Horse**: Hold by rein the rein -> Look 10 steps forward -> Walk the horse. Don't look at the horse it will stop. Hold with a fist thumbs closed.
	1. Stop the Horse: Stop walking and pull the rein slightly backwards
	2. Turn the Horse towards your standing direction: Slightly pull the rein towards the direction
	3. Turn the Horse to the opposite direction from your standing position: Take the thumb out through the circular bit and place it over the horse's chin, then slowly move towards the other opposite direction
4. The horse might shake their head or stomp if irritated by flies or other irritants. In that case keep holding the rein, once the horse calms down pat on the forehead.

#### Walking Embrosia while Mounted
1. **Standing Position while Mounting**: Stand parallel to the Horse's shoulder
2. **Mounting**: Put the left leg in the Stirrup, Hold the Pommel and Cantle and climb the horse.
3. **Dismounting**: Put both hands on the Horse's neck and slide down towards the left
![[horse_saddle.png|450]]
4. **Types of Riding Styles**
	1. English Style
	2. Western Style
	3. Racing Style
5. **English Style - Seating Position while Mounted on Horse**
	1. **Hand Position:** Hold the reins three fingers in with little finger out - this is to ensure good grip. Both hands thumbs together with fists pointing downwards. Do not rest the fists on the saddle.
		1. Loosen the Rein: Feist both the fists upwards while holding the rein
		2. Tighten the Rein: Use index finger and thumb to hold the opposite rein, then slip the fist downwards. Same with the other side.
	2. **Leg Position**: Rest the ball joints of the toes on the Stirrup. This avoids accidents due to easy exit out of the saddle if need be. Knees slightly bent to take the feet backwards. Heels resting down.
	3. **Body Position**: Sit upright with spine straight. The shoulder should be inline with the feet.
