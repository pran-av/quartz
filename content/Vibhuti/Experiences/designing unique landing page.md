I am making Figma designs for the elevator pitch app and I am a bit stuck right now. Thinking what content should be added and shall I make a regular SaaS style website or try something new.

Like since the inspiration for "Elevator Pitch" itself comes from elevators - like in a sense you are in an elevator with someone and you have to describe your idea within the few seconds the elevator takes to reach the destination floor.

So like pitch in 60 seconds. Give context of the who you are, how you going to solve the problem, what you need from them.

In context of the app - I could write "create your own Elevator Pitch within in minute". Now some people would not know what elevator pitches are. What to do then?

Maybe when the cursor hovers the word or when the user clicks on it in mobile I would show an info box.
