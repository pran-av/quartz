a [[Mineral]]. An aluminium silicate.

Mica has a layered structure that allows it to split into thin sheets.

![[MicaStructure.png| 450]]
#### Types
1. Muscovite - light coloured rich in potassium
2. Biotite - dark coloured rich in iron and magnesium
3. Phlogopite - additional magnesium compared to biotite

#### Applications
1. Electronics - as insulator due to [[Dielectric]] properties
2. Cosmetics - shimmering effect and ability to reflect light
